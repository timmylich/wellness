<p>Wellness is a free, open-source health tracker that keeps all your data on your phone. No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no Google services.</p>
<p>Every section can be turned off, and the app adapts to you: personal nutrient limits, step goal, water buttons, theme, colour and language. What you do not need stays out of the way.</p>
<p><b>FOOD</b></p>
<ul><li>12 daily scales: calories, protein, fat, carbs, sugar, added sugar, sodium, fibre, saturated and trans fat, cholesterol, potassium. Personal targets based on your profile, goal and activity.</li><li>Offline food database, Open Food Facts and USDA search, barcodes, counting by pieces, custom foods.</li><li>Weekly energy balance and nutrition advice.</li><li>Forgot to log a day? Fill it in with an estimate: as usual, little, a lot, sweet, fatty, high-protein.</li></ul>
<p><b>WATER</b></p>
<ul><li>One tap for water and a separate button for other drinks, charts, the recommended range and a clearly marked dangerous amount.</li></ul>
<p><b>ACTIVITY</b></p>
<ul><li>Background step counting with the phone&#x27;s sensor, or Health Connect.</li><li>Workout templates with calorie estimates.</li><li>Reminders such as &quot;walk 8000 steps&quot; or &quot;warm-up every 2 days&quot;. Already done? No reminder.</li></ul>
<p><b>HEART AND BODY</b></p>
<ul><li>Blood pressure and pulse with ACC/AHA classification and heart-rate zones.</li><li>Weight trend, body fat estimate, BMI, waist-to-height ratio, healthy weight range.</li><li>Charts on a real time scale: a month between readings looks longer than three days.</li></ul>
<p><b>PANTRY (optional)</b></p>
<ul><li>Expiry dates, a freshness bar, opened packages, how much is left and a reminder the day before a product expires.</li><li>Eaten or thrown away — all of it or part, with waste statistics.</li><li>Dish ideas from what you have at home, starting with what expires soon.</li><li>A shared pantry and shopping list for the family through one code: no accounts, end-to-end encrypted.</li><li>Ready-made prompts for AI chats with your product list.</li></ul>
<p><b>TIPS</b></p>
<ul><li>Fresh air, drinking water, cancer prevention, sun, back health, sleep and nutrition, with sources.</li></ul>
<p>Internet is optional: food search, the shared pantry and the update check. Wellness is not a medical device.</p>
