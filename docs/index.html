Wellness is a native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose app: no JavaScript engine, no heavy SDKs, hand-drawn charts and smooth swipes even on budget phones. It works fully offline.

Everything for everyday health in one app

Calorie and nutrient counter

12 daily scales — calories, protein, fat, carbs, sugar, added sugar, sodium, fibre, saturated and trans fat, cholesterol, potassium — with a personal minimum, target and maximum based on your profile, goal pace and activity.

Quick food logging without AI

Recent foods, favourites, an offline database (CIS dishes and ~2300 European foods from ANSES-CIQUAL), Open Food Facts and USDA search, barcodes, counting by pieces and custom foods.

Energy balance

Intake versus expenditure (BMR × lifestyle + workouts + steps), with surplus or deficit converted into estimated fat mass and optional adaptive targets.

Water tracker

One big button for water and one for other drinks, charts up to a year, a recommended range and a clearly marked dangerous amount.

Step counter and workouts

Background step counting with the phone's hardware sensor — no Google Fit needed — or Health Connect, plus MET-based workout templates.

Blood pressure and pulse

A blood pressure log with ACC/AHA classification and heart-rate zones.

Weight and body composition

Weight trend with a time-weighted average, body fat (measured, US Navy or BMI-based), BMI, waist-to-height ratio and a healthy weight range.

Pantry and shopping list

What you have at home, expiry dates, opened packages, a reminder before a product expires, a shopping list and eaten / thrown-away statistics. Share it with your family through one code, end-to-end encrypted.

Health tips with sources

Fresh air, water, cancer prevention, sun, back health, sleep and nutrition. Every formula and limit cites WHO, IOM/EFSA, ACC/AHA or USDA and is covered by tests.

Your data stays on your phone

No accounts

No sign-up, no e-mail, no cloud profile. Everything is stored in one file on the device.

Network only when you ask

Food search, the update check and the shared pantry are optional and can be turned off. Nothing else goes online.

Your backups

JSON export and import with a reminder every 30 days — save it to your drive or send it to a messenger. Privacy policy.

Install

Download the APK from GitHub Releases and open it on your phone, allowing installs from your browser or file manager when asked. The app can notify you about new versions. An F-Droid listing is planned.

FAQ

Is Wellness really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no paid features. It is free software under the GNU AGPL v3 license.

Does it work without Google Play services?

Yes. It runs on any Android 8.0+ phone, including de-Googled ROMs such as LineageOS, GrapheneOS and /e/OS.

Does it need the internet?

No. All features work offline. Online food search and the shared pantry are optional.

Which languages are supported?

English and Russian.

Is it a medical device?

No. Recommendations are general guidelines for healthy adults and do not replace a doctor's advice.
